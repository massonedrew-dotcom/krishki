## Found during review, NOT caused by this ticket
1. **HIGH — the mobile menu is broken.** In both `before-menu-390.png` and `after-menu-390.png`, only «Продукция» is visible, drawn over the hero; the panel itself is not visible. The cause is `.site-header` at `site.css:183`, which has `backdrop-filter`. That makes the header the containing block for the `position: fixed` `.menu-panel` (`site.css:271`). The panel is then sized to the header (`inset: var(--header-h) 0 0 0`, which gives zero height) and the links overflow. Fix: render the panel outside `.site-header` (portal to `body`), or move the blur to a `::before` pseudo-element of the header. Needs its own ticket. Plan 007 cannot be feel-checked until this is fixed.
2. LOW — `pnpm lint` picks up `apps/web/out/`. Add `out/**` to the ignores in `apps/web/eslint.config.mjs`.
