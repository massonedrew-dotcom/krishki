## SKO I-82 (крышка СКО I-82)
**Factory (confirmed, catalogue slides and old site):**
- For glass jars with the SKO I-82 neck finish (crown neck, Ø 82 mm, closed with a seaming key).
- Tin: ЭЖК (electrolytic tinplate). Thickness: 0.17–0.20 mm for «Мечта хозяйки» / «Елисейские крышки», 0.16 mm for «Моя мечта» / «Роса»; tin coating 2.8/2.8.
- Coating, outside: gold single-colour lacquer, or full-colour lithography. Inside: food enamel and lacquer, silver-grey.
- Lacquers and inks: outer ЭП 570/С (RU), inner enamel ЭП 5147/С (RU), top coat PPG 2040 (DE), inks HUBER GROUP (DE). These are the «Моя мечта» / «Роса» lines; the old site says PPG lacquers in general.
- Food-grade rubber sealing ring, plus an extra stiffening rib.
- Packaging: tube of 50. «Мечта хозяйки»: 10 tubes = 500 per bag. «Елисейские»: 1,000 per box. «Моя мечта» / «Роса»: 1,000 per box (white box / box with logo).

**Industry background (do not publish numbers without the client):**
- Retail SKO 1-82 lids are commonly made of 0.18 / 0.20 / 0.22 mm tinplate. Ours is 0.16–0.20, which is the factory's own figure.
- Some references give the SKO-1-82 lid outer Ø as 89.6 mm and height as 6.9 mm. This is not confirmed for our lids: TODO(client), with a drawing.
- No single GOST number for the lid was found in open sources. Glass jar necks are covered by ГОСТ 32131-2021 (jars for food). Do not cite a GOST on the site until the client names theirs: TODO(client).

## Twist-off 82 (твист-офф 82, lug cap)
**Factory (confirmed):**
- Screw lid for jars with an 82 threaded neck (commercial name of the neck finish: ТО-82). Tin: ЭЖК canning tinplate.
- Outside: bright single-colour lithography and lacquer (green, red in the catalogue) or full-colour. Inside: lacquer.
- Central vacuum button for visual and audible control. Plastisol gasket for sterilisation and pasteurisation. Closes by hand, no seaming machine needed.
- Packaging: tube of 20. 12 tubes = 240 per bag. Bulk box of 850 for canneries.

**Industry background:**
- A lug cap mates with tapered lugs on the neck; a partial turn seals it. Plastisol liners are the standard for hot-fill and pasteurisation; supplier data sheets differ on process limits. So the site says only «под стерилизацию и пастеризацию», as the factory does, with no temperatures.
- The usual international designation for this size is 82 mm lug. The number of lugs on our lid is unknown (the 3D model assumes 4; many 82 mm caps have 4–6): TODO(client).
